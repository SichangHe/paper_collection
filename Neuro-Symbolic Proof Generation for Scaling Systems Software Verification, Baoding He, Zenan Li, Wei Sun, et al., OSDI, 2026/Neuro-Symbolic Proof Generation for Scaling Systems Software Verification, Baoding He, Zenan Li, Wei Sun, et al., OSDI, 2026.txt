Figure 2: Overview of our neuro-symbolic proof-search framework. Starting from the initial proof state, the system repeatedly
generates candidate proof steps using a fine-tuned LLM, followed by rule-based symbolic revision to repair syntactic or semantic
issues in the proposed steps. Each accepted step is executed by Isabelle/HOL to derive new proof states, which are then filtered
by symbolic tools to prune states containing counterexamples or duplicates. The remaining states are ranked using cumulative
LLM log-probabilities, and the highest-scoring ones are selected for further expansion in a best-first search. If the search cannot
complete the proof, the framework finally invokes Sledgehammer to attempt to resolve the remaining goals.
